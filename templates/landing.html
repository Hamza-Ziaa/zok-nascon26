How We Check The News

The 4-Pillar Credibility Standard

We never guess. Every headline passes an open, transparent credibility check before it enters your paper.

35%
Publisher record

Newsrooms with strict editorial standards and fact-checking history, such as Reuters, BBC, Dawn, AP and DW.

30%
Independent confirmation

A story is matched against other newsrooms. If only one outlet reports it, the score drops until others confirm.

20%
Real evidence

Named officials, statements, documents and data raise the score. Clickbait and rumours lower it.

15%
Freshness and attribution

Under 48 hours old, with a working link and clear attribution.
