How We Check The News

The 4-Pillar Credibility Standard

We never guess. Every headline is evaluated through an open, transparent credibility engine before it enters your paper.

35%
Publisher Trust

We prioritize newsrooms with strict editorial standards and fact-checking records — such as Reuters, BBC, Dawn, Associated Press, and DW.

30%
Independent Sources

A major story is checked against multiple news organizations. If only one blog is claiming it, the score drops until corroborated.

20%
Real Evidence & Quotes

We scan for named officials, official statements, court documents, and statistical surveys, while heavily penalizing clickbait and rumors.

15%
Freshness & Attribution

Stories must be under 48 hours old with a working canonical link, transparent bylines, and zero dead redirects.
